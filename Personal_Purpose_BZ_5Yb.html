Five layers of why

A root-cause analysis of the mission statement — what drives each dimension at its deepest level.

1
Mission · Dimension 1
To LeadWhy do you lead?
  • 1
    Freedom
    You value the freedom to make decisions and own responsibility for them.
  • 2
    Cognitive Power
    Your understanding and cognitive abilities lead to better outcomes when you lead.
  • 3
    Proven Results
    Experience confirms you achieve better results when you are in the lead.
  • 4
    Obligation
    A personal sense of duty compels you to step into leadership roles.
  • 5
    Lifelong Identity
    Since childhood, internal and external forces have positioned you as a leader.
2
Mission · Dimension 2
Ambitious TransformationsWhy ambition and transformation?
  • 1
    Creative Drive
    You want to create and build — not merely manage what already exists.
  • 2
    Growth as Driver
    Growth is the key engine of wealth creation and positive societal change.
  • 3
    Economic Impact
    Transformations generate economic surplus that benefits society broadly.
  • 4
    Innovation Focus
    Innovation and transformation are the mechanisms of meaningful, lasting change.
  • 5
    Seniority Alignment
    As your career has matured, the scale of ambition you seek has grown with it.
3
Mission · Dimension 3
Of Relevant BusinessesWhy businesses — and why relevant ones?
  • 1
    Natural Arena
    You lead in business contexts — making companies the logical stage for impact.
  • 2
    Desire for Impact
    Relevant, sizable businesses maximise the reach and depth of your influence.
  • 3
    Scale of Change
    Larger businesses amplify the impact on employees, customers, and society.
  • 4
    Career Criteria
    Size and relevance are decisive filters in evaluating every opportunity.
  • 5
    Legacy Requirement
    Only relevant businesses create the conditions for a lasting, visible legacy.
4
Mission · Dimension 4
And Their PeopleWhy does the human dimension matter?
  • 1
    Greatest Motivation
    Developing people — especially top talent — is your deepest source of energy.
  • 2
    Need for Bonding
    A strong inner need for deep, trust-based relationships shapes how you lead.
  • 3
    Value of Trust
    Relationships built on trust and love are among the most valuable things in life.
  • 4
    Ultimate Fulfillment
    Love and happiness are the ultimate goals — these relationships are central to both.
  • 5
    Empathetic Identity
    Unconditional kindness is inseparable from who you are as a leader and a person.
